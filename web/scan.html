DebugPilot

Paste a website. Find every crash. Get a fix that's proven in the live page.

ChecksCrashes + Speed
Read-only scan for UPS, PDU, cooling and switchgear web pages on your network
  • Only opens pages and follows links. Never presses buttons or types anything.
  • Never opens control pages (Shutdown, Reboot, Outlets, Self test, Settings).
  • Blocks anything the page tries to send, so no setting or command can reach the device.
